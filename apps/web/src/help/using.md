# Using SaudaAI every day

SaudaAI answers your customers and keeps your store up to date. You talk to it in Telegram or in the box at the top of the dashboard, by text, voice note or photo, in Bangla, Banglish or English.

## What it does without asking

- Answers customers on Messenger, Instagram, WhatsApp and your website chat: price, sizes, colors, stock, delivery charge, order status.
- Sends the product photo from your store when a customer asks to see it.
- Collects the order details from the customer (item, size, quantity, name, mobile number, address, payment method) and drafts the order.
- Sends a customer their online payment link when they ask for it.

It never offers a discount, a refund or a special promise. If a customer asks for one, it tells you.

## What waits for your tap

These come to Telegram (and the Approvals page) with Approve and Cancel buttons:

- Confirming an order. On approval the order is created in your store and the customer gets the confirmation and invoice.
- Recording a payment, and payment reminders.
- Cancelling an order.
- A new product made from your photo.
- Posting an ad on your Facebook Page, Instagram or website.
- A reply to a customer that mentions a number SaudaAI could not find in your shop data.

In Settings you can let some of these run without asking, for example confirming small orders from customer chats.

## Where to look in the dashboard

- **Today**: sales, orders waiting, low stock.
- **Approvals**: everything waiting for your tap.
- **Analytics**: sales by channel, what customers asked for, how much the assistant handled.
- **Orders, Catalog, Customers**: what is in your store, kept in sync.
- **Inbox**: every customer conversation. Open one and press "I will answer" to reply yourself; the assistant stays quiet for two hours or until you press "Let the assistant answer".
- **Posts**: ads and banners that were published.
- **Agent log**: every step the assistant took and why.
- **Settings**: your store, delivery charges, payment methods, what may run without asking, website chat snippet, channels.

## Telegram commands

- `/summary`: today's summary.
- `/reply <chat> <message>`: send a message to a customer as the shop. The chat id is in the Telegram message about that customer.
- `/take <chat>` and `/resume <chat>`: answer a conversation yourself for two hours, and hand it back.
- `/login`: a code to sign in to the dashboard.

A summary of the day arrives every night at 22:00.
